# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 4, 2026

This privacy policy describes how curiosityworks.org ("we", "us") handles personal
information in connection with its SMS/text messaging program.

## Scope of the messaging program

The text messaging program is used for **personal notifications sent to the site
owner only**. Messages are automated alerts generated by systems the site owner
runs (for example, build, deployment, or monitoring notifications).

We do not send marketing or promotional text messages, and we do not send text
messages to students, visitors, or any other third party.

## Information we collect

For the messaging program we collect and store only:

- The mobile phone number of the site owner, which is the sole recipient.
- The content and delivery status of messages sent to that number, as retained by
  our messaging provider.

We do not collect phone numbers from visitors to this site, and this site does not
offer a form or other mechanism to sign up for text messages.

## How we use this information

The phone number is used solely to deliver the notification messages described
above. Message content is used only to convey the notification and to troubleshoot
delivery problems.

## How we share this information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for
marketing purposes.

**Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for
marketing or promotional purposes.** Information may be shared only with the
service providers that are necessary to deliver the messages — specifically
Twilio, our messaging provider, which transmits messages on our behalf and is
subject to its own privacy policy at
[https://www.twilio.com/legal/privacy](https://www.twilio.com/legal/privacy).
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third
party.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law or valid legal
process.

## Data retention

Message records are retained by our messaging provider according to that
provider's standard retention period, and are deleted when no longer needed for
the purpose described above.

## Consent, opt-in, and opt-out

The recipient is the site owner, who has provided consent for these messages by
configuring the notification system with their own phone number.

You may stop messages at any time by replying **STOP** to any message. You will
receive a single confirmation and no further messages will be sent. Reply
**HELP** for assistance, or contact us using the details below.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not
liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Children's privacy

The messaging program is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this
page with a revised "Last updated" date.
